# MarketLink

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product and audience

MarketLink connects local businesses with nearby marketing providers. The audience is not restricted to retail shops or to one named city. Providers include agencies, freelancers, creators, and specialists.

## Primary job

Find suitable nearby marketing help, compare provider services and past work, and contact a provider directly. Users may know the service they need or start from a business goal without knowing marketing terminology.

## Confirmed discovery flow

The founder describes an Uber Eats-style local discovery experience for marketing providers. The landing page explains the offer and exposes five prominent marketing-service entry points. The browse page combines service/skill filters, provider results, and a location map. A provider details page explains that provider's services, work, and contact options. Signed-in users also have role-specific dashboards. This discovery and map implementation already exists; previews must not be presented as a production rebuild.

The experience should be accessible and easy to understand for adults from roughly 20 through 60 and older. Use readable text, clear labels, visible focus, strong contrast, and generous interaction targets. The founder's current visual preference is more modern typography and layout, with illustrations supporting the product rather than substituting for it.

The homepage must lead with finding a local marketing team or agency for the business's overall marketing needs. SEO, Google Ads, social media, branding, and websites are expertise filters for choosing providers; a selected service must not make the homepage look like MarketLink sells that service itself. Design comparisons should keep this product positioning constant and vary only the presentation. The founder prefers three preview options before any live visual change.

The founder selected preview B (the navy introduction and agency showcase) on 2026-11-03, requesting an additional agency-results-and-map Explore section below the showcase, like preview C. Keep that selected direction rather than creating further alternatives. The homepage implements the selected preview styles with clearly labeled sample agencies and an illustrative map. Real results, filters, and the live map remain in Browse experts. No production deployment is implied.

## Business model and boundaries

Providers pay MarketLink a subscription. Businesses and providers arrange the scope, deal, and payment directly. Do not imply MarketLink processes project payments or guarantees business results. Do not invent testimonials, provider counts, quality scores, prices, or verification claims.

## Constraints

The existing frontend is Next.js App Router, React, TypeScript, and Tailwind; the backend is Fastify and Prisma. Use npm. Preserve working behavior, permissions, and accessibility. The founder has authorized implementing preview B and matching its visual design.
